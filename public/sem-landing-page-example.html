How long does braces treatment take? +

Braces treatment typically takes 12-24 months depending on the complexity of your case. Regular adjustments every 4-6 weeks are required. Dr. Achla Verma will provide a personalized treatment timeline during your consultation at our Sector 65, Gurgaon clinic.

Which is better: metal braces or Invisalign? +

Both metal braces and Invisalign are effective for straightening teeth. Metal braces are more affordable and suitable for complex cases. Invisalign is nearly invisible and removable, making it popular among adults. Dr. Achla Verma will recommend the best option based on your specific needs during consultation.

Do braces hurt? +

You may experience some discomfort when braces are first placed and after adjustments, but this is usually mild and temporary. Most patients adjust within a few days. We provide care instructions and can recommend pain relief if needed.

Can I get braces at any age? +

Yes! Braces can be effective at any age. While orthodontic treatment is often associated with teenagers, many adults successfully complete braces treatment. We treat patients of all ages at our Sector 65, Gurgaon clinic.

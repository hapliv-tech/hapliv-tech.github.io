Frequently Asked Questions About LASER Dentistry

What is LASER dentistry and how does it work?

LASER dentistry uses focused light beams to perform precise dental procedures. The laser energy can cut, shape, or remove tissue with minimal discomfort. LASER dental treatment is used for gum contouring, teeth whitening, disinfection, and various soft tissue procedures. It offers a gentle, minimally invasive alternative to traditional dental methods.

Is LASER dentistry safe?

Yes, LASER dentistry is FDA-approved and safe when performed by trained professionals. Our expert dentists at Hapliv Dental Clinic use advanced laser technology with proper safety protocols. LASER dental treatment reduces bleeding, minimizes discomfort, and promotes faster healing compared to conventional methods.

What is the cost of LASER dentistry in Gurgaon?

The cost of LASER dentistry in Gurgaon varies by procedure. Laser gingival procedures range from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, while laser-assisted whitening typically ranges ₹10,000 to ₹20,000. Final pricing depends on the specific treatment and individual case evaluation. Contact us for a detailed consultation and personalized quote.

Does LASER dentistry hurt?

LASER dentistry is often very comfortable and may need less anesthesia than traditional methods. Most patients experience minimal discomfort during laser dental treatment. The procedure is faster, causes less bleeding, and promotes faster healing, making it a calm option for dental care.

What LASER dental treatments are available at Hapliv Dental Clinic?

We offer various LASER dental treatments including laser gum contouring/gingivectomy, laser teeth whitening, laser disinfection in root canal and periodontal treatment, laser ulcer healing, laser TMJ treatment, and laser smile design. Our expert dentists in Sector 65, Gurgaon and West Delhi provide comprehensive laser dental care.

How long does LASER dental treatment take?

LASER dental treatment is typically faster than traditional methods. Most laser procedures take 30-60 minutes depending on the complexity. For example, laser gum contouring may take 30-45 minutes, while laser teeth whitening can be completed in 60-90 minutes. Recovery time is also significantly reduced.

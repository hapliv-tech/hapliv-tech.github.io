<h3 id="1-schedule-regular-dental-check-ups-every-6-months">1. Schedule Regular Dental Check-ups Every 6 Months</h3>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Regular check-ups catch problems early, when they&#39;re easier and less expensive to treat.</p>
<p><strong>How to achieve it:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Book your next appointment before leaving the clinic</li>
<li>Set calendar reminders for 6 months ahead</li>

<hr>
<h3 id="2-master-proper-brushing-and-flossing-technique">2. Master Proper Brushing and Flossing Technique</h3>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Proper technique removes plaque effectively and prevents gum disease and cavities.</p>
<p><strong>How to achieve it:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Use a soft-bristled toothbrush at a 45-degree angle</li>
<li>Brush for 2 minutes, twice daily (morning and before bed)</li>
<li>Floss daily, using proper technique (C-shape around each tooth)</li>
<li>Replace your toothbrush every 3-4 months</li>
<li>Consider an electric toothbrush for better plaque removal</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Action plan:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Ask your dentist to demonstrate proper technique at your next visit</li>
<li>Set a timer for 2 minutes while brushing</li>
<li>Keep floss visible (on your nightstand or bathroom counter)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Benefit:</strong> Prevents 80% of dental problems with proper daily care.</p>

<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Smoking is one of the leading causes of gum disease, tooth loss, and oral cancer.</p>

<ul>
<li>Stained teeth and bad breath</li>
<li>Increased risk of gum disease</li>
<li>Slower healing after dental procedures</li>
<li>Higher risk of oral cancer</li>
<li>Reduced success rate of dental implants</li>

<p><strong>How to achieve it:</strong></p>
<ul>

<li>Consider nicotine replacement therapy</li>

<p><strong>Foods to limit:</strong></p>

<li>Sticky, chewy candies</li>
<li>Carbonated drinks</li>

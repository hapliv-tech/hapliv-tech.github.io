## Invisalign Treatment Timeline at Hapliv Dental Clinic

Every smile is unique, yet most patients follow a similar rhythm once they start aligners. This month-by-month outline helps you plan time off, reviews, and lifestyle tweaks.

- **Pre-treatment (Week 0-2):** 3D iTero scan, bite analysis, and ClinCheck simulation to map the exact number of aligners. We also address cavities or gum concerns before starting.
- **Month 1:** First set of aligners is fitted, attachments are bonded, and you receive wear-and-care training. Expect slight pressure for the first 48 hours of each new tray.
- **Month 2-3:** Aligners are changed every 7-10 days. Remote check-ins via WhatsApp or the My Invisalign app help us verify progress. Minor interproximal reduction (IPR) may be scheduled for crowding cases.
- **Month 4-6:** Mid-treatment review at the clinic to compare your progress with the digital plan. Refinement scans are taken if the teeth need small course corrections.
- **Month 7-9:** Final aligners close micro-spaces and perfect the bite. Attachments are polished off once alignment stabilises.
- **Retention Phase:** You receive Vivera or custom night-time retainers to protect the hard work you invested in your smile.

## Habits That Keep Invisalign on Track

Consistent wear (20-22 hours per day) accounts for 80% of your results. Pair that discipline with these proven tips:

- Rinse and brush your trays with lukewarm water whenever you remove them to prevent plaque film.
- Use OrthoFoam or aligner cleaning crystals twice a week to keep trays crystal clear.
- Stick to soft foods for the first 24 hours after switching trays; introduce crunchy items once tenderness settles.
- Log tray changes in your phone calendar so you never extend a stage longer than prescribed.
- Bring your storage case everywhere—napkins and pockets are top reasons aligners get lost.

## When to Contact Your Orthodontist

Reach out to us quickly if:

- An attachment or button pops off.
- A tray feels unusually sharp or fails to seat even after using chewies.
- You lose a tray mid-stage—never skip ahead without guidance.
- Your gums look inflamed or you experience sustained jaw discomfort.

Prompt touchpoints keep your smile from drifting off plan and ensure refinements stay minimal.

Starting braces is more than a cosmetic decision—it is a structured journey that blends orthodontic science with daily habits. Whether you are choosing treatment for yourself or your child, understanding what lies ahead removes surprises and keeps motivation high. This guide breaks down the Hapliv Dental Clinic experience month by month, highlighting comfort tips, diet suggestions, and ways to maximise every adjustment.

## Before the Braces Go On

Your first consultation includes digital X-rays, photographs, and a bite analysis. We discuss aesthetic preferences, work or school schedules, and whether you prefer metal, ceramic, or self-ligating braces. Once you confirm the plan:

- Professional cleaning and fluoride polish prepare teeth for brackets.
- Any pending fillings, sealants, or extractions are completed.
- An impression or 3D scan maps your arches, helping us position brackets precisely.

![Orthodontist discussing braces options with a patient at Hapliv Dental Clinic](/assets/blog/child_braces.webp)

## Month 1: Placement and Adapting

- Brackets are bonded, archwires are secured, and we share wax, ortho brushes, and emergency protocols.
- Expect mild soreness for three to four days—stick to lukewarm soups, khichdi, smoothies, and yoghurt.
- Brush after every meal with a soft-bristle orthodontic brush and finish with a fluoride mouthwash.

> Pro tip: Combine over-the-counter pain relievers (if approved by your physician) with paraffin wax on sharp edges to sail through week one comfortably.

## Month 2-4: Building Healthy Habits

- Adjustment appointments happen every 4-6 weeks to tighten archwires, add power chains, or place coil springs.
- We review your cleaning score at each visit; patients who maintain 0 bleeding points drastically cut treatment time.
- If elastics are prescribed, wear them exactly as directed to avoid bite relapse.

## Month 5-8: Noticing Smile Changes

- Teeth rotations and crowding begin to resolve; you will start seeing the braces investment pay off.
- Dietary discipline matters—avoid popcorn, chewy mithai, and hard candies that can dislodge brackets.
- Schedule your professional cleaning every 6 months to keep gums healthy during tooth movement.

![Girl smiling confidently with braces after mid-treatment adjustment](/assets/blog/girl-smiling-with-braces.webp)

## Month 9-12: Fine-Tuning the Bite

- We focus on closing micro-gaps and balancing your bite so that upper and lower teeth meet harmoniously.
- Expect elastics or bite turbos to fine-tune jaw relationships.
- Ceramic brace wearers may switch to lighter archwires to maintain aesthetics while final adjustments happen.

## Beyond 12 Months: Debonding and Retention

- Once alignment and bite goals are met, brackets come off and your teeth are polished.
- We fit fixed lingual retainers or removable vacuum-formed trays depending on your case.
- Night-time retainer wear protects your investment; we schedule annual reviews to guard against relapse.

## Keep Your Braces Journey Smooth

- Carry an ortho kit with interdental brushes, floss threaders, and wax in your bag or school locker.
- Sip water frequently; it washes away food particles that can stain ceramic brackets.
- Replace your toothbrush every 6-8 weeks—braces wear bristles faster.
- Book an emergency slot immediately if you notice loose wires, poking brackets, or mouth ulcers.

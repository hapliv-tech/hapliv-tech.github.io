Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental Implants are the best permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. At Hapliv Dental Clinic in Sector 65, Gurgaon & West Delhi, our expert implantologist provides dental implants that look, feel, and function like your natural teeth.

Our dental implants are made from biocompatible titanium that fuses with your jawbone, providing a stable foundation for replacement teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implantspreserve bone health and prevent jawbone deterioration.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Permanent tooth replacement solution
  • Natural look and feel
  • Preserves jawbone health
  • No damage to adjacent teeth
  • Long-lasting (often lifetime)
  • Restores full chewing function

Types of Dental Implants

  • Single Tooth Implant - Replace one missing tooth
  • Multiple Implants - Replace several missing teeth
  • Full Mouth Implants - Complete tooth replacement
  • Implant-Supported Bridge - Multiple teeth on fewer implants

Dental Implant Treatment Process

1

Consultation & Planning

Our expert implantologist evaluates your oral health, takes X-rays and 3D scans, and creates a personalized treatment plan for your dental implants.

2

Implant Placement

Titanium implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia. The procedure is straightforward and minimally invasive for most patients.

3

Healing & Integration

The implant fuses with your jawbone over 3-6 months (osseointegration), creating a strong foundation for your replacement tooth.

4

Abutment Placement

After healing, an abutment is attached to the implant, which will hold your custom crown or bridge.

5

Crown/Bridge Placement

Your custom-made crown or bridge is placed on the abutment, completing your dental implant treatment with a natural-looking result.

6

Follow-up Care

Regular check-ups ensure your dental implants remain healthy and functional for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

What is the cost of dental implants in Gurgaon?

The cost of dental implants in Gurgaon ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹70,000 per implant depending on the type and complexity. Single implant (post only) costs ₹25,000-₹50,000, while single implant with crown costs ₹35,000-₹70,000. Final pricing depends on bone condition, number of implants, and crown material. Contact us for a detailed consultation and personalized quote.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. The titanium implant post fuses with your jawbone (osseointegration) and becomes a permanent part of your mouth. With good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, dental implants can last 20-30 years or more. The crown may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

Dental implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Most patients report minimal discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction. Post-surgery, mild swelling and discomfort may occur for 2-3 days, which can be managed with prescribed medications. Our expert implantologist ensures a comfortable experience.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The complete dental implant process typically takes 3-6 months. After implant placement, there is a healing period of 3-6 months for osseointegration (bone fusion). Once healed, the abutment and crown are placed, which takes 2-4 weeks. The timeline may vary based on individual healing and bone condition.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Good candidates for dental implants have healthy gums, adequate jawbone density, and good overall health. Patients with uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smokers, or those with insufficient bone may need additional procedures. Our expert implantologist in Sector 65, Gurgaon will evaluate your condition and determine if dental implants are right for you.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of 95-98% when performed by experienced implantologists. Success depends on proper placement, good oral hygiene, and patient health. At Hapliv Dental Clinic, our expert implantologist uses advanced techniques and quality implants to ensure optimal results.

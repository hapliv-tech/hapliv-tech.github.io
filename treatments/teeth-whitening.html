Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening safely lightens tooth shade by removing stains from enamel and dentin. Our expert dental surgeons in Sector 65, Gurgaon and West Delhi provide this treatment with advanced technology and painless procedures.

Types / Options

Choose the option that best fits your goals and lifestyle.

In-Office Whitening
  • High-performance gels with light activation.
  • Fast, visible results in a single visit.
Take-Home Trays
  • Custom trays with dentist-supervised gels.
  • Gradual whitening over 1-2 weeks.

What to Expect

A clear, step-by-step journey designed for comfort and great results.

Before Treatment

  • Oral exam; cleaning may be advised.
  • Shade recording and photos.

During Treatment

  • Gum protection and gel application.
  • 1-3 activation cycles depending on protocol.

After Treatment

  • Temporary sensitivity possible for 24-48 hours.
  • Maintenance advice to prolong results.

Food Guidelines

Eat smart during and after treatment for better comfort.

Avoid

  • Strong chromogenic foods/drinks for 48 hours (tea, coffee, wine, beetroot)
  • Smoking

Prefer

  • 'White diet': milk, rice, bananas, plain yogurt

Tip

Use sensitivity toothpaste before/after treatment if prone to sensitivity.

Choosing Not to Get Treatment - Pros & Cons

Whitening is a safe, quick boost to smile aesthetics when professionally supervised.

Pros

  • No sensitivity or dietary restrictions
  • No cost/time investment

Cons

  • Persistent stains affect smile aesthetics
  • Loss of confidence for events/photography

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe for enamel?

Yes—professional whitening is safe when dentist-supervised.

How long do results last?

3-6 months depending on diet and habits; touch-ups help maintain shade.

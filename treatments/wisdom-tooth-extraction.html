          Wisdom Tooth Extraction

          Removal of impacted or problematic third molars to prevent pain, infection, and crowding.

          Call NowBook Consultation

          Types / Options

          Choose the option that best fits your goals and lifestyle.

          Simple Extraction
          • For erupted wisdom teeth with sufficient access.
          • Quick procedure under local anesthesia.
          Surgical Extraction
          • For impacted/partially erupted teeth.
          • Flap elevation and sectioning when needed.

          Wisdom Tooth Extraction cost in Gurgaon & Delhi

          Varies with impaction depth, root anatomy, and need for surgical flap.

          TypeRange
          Simple Extraction₹2,000 - ₹4,000
          Surgical Extraction₹5,000 - ₹10,000

          What to Expect

          A clear, step-by-step journey designed for comfort and great results.

          Before Treatment

          • Panoramic X-ray/CBCT if indicated.
          • Medical history and consent; post-op instructions explained.

          During Treatment

          • Local anesthesia; atraumatic extraction.
          • Stitches may be placed; gauze pressure for hemostasis.

          After Treatment

          • Swelling peaks at 48-72 hours; use cold compress first 24 hours.
          • Sutures removed in 5-7 days if non-resorbable; follow-up review.

          Food Guidelines

          Eat smart during and after treatment for better comfort.

          Avoid

          • Straws and spitting (48 hours) to prevent dry socket
          • Hard/spicy foods; smoking/alcohol in early healing

          Prefer

          • Cold, soft foods (curd, smoothies, khichdi, soups)
          • Plenty of fluids

          Tip

          Start warm saline rinses after 24 hours; follow medication schedule strictly.

          Choosing Not to Get Treatment - Pros & Cons

          Timely removal prevents recurrent infections and protects neighboring teeth.

          Pros

          • No surgery or downtime if asymptomatic

          Cons

          • Recurrent pericoronitis (gum infection) and pain
          • Decay in adjacent molars due to food trapping
          • Cyst formation or crowding (in select cases)

          Frequently Asked Questions

          Will it hurt?

          The area is numbed; you’ll feel pressure but not pain. Post-op soreness is managed with medications.

          How long is recovery?

          Most patients resume routine in 1–3 days; complete healing takes a couple of weeks.
